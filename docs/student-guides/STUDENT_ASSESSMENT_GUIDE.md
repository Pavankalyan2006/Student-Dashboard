# Assessment Guide

- **Quiz** (`/quiz`): new quizzes are created with AI from a topic, your notes or a document. If AI is off, you cannot
  create a new quiz yet (known pilot limitation). Scores are calculated on the server, and answers stay hidden until you
  submit. After submitting you can review each answer with its explanation, and Quiz progress shows your history.
- **Exam Preparation** (`/exams`), **GATE Exam Preparation** (`/assessment/gate-preparation`): practise questions and track
  attempts.
- **Mock Interviews** (`/interviews` or `/mock-interviews`): set up a session for a role. AI feedback needs AI to be on.
- **Placement Preparation** (`/placement`): aptitude, coding, technical and HR practice.

Troubleshooting: if a quiz does not submit, do not refresh. Wait a moment and press Submit again; your answers are kept.
Report repeated failures in Pilot Feedback.

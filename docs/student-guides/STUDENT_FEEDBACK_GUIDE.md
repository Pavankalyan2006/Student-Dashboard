# Feedback Guide

Open menu > Profile > **Pilot Feedback** (`/pilot-feedback`), or Settings > Privacy & Data > Open pilot feedback.

Choose one:
- **Survey**: rate what you used from 1 (Very poor) to 5 (Excellent). Leave a question empty if you did not use it. Tick the
  tasks you tried and, if you like, add a comment.
- **Problem report**: what you were doing (module and page), what happened, what you expected, and steps to repeat it.
- **Privacy or security concern**: anything that looks private but was visible, or anything that worries you.

Before sending, read the notice and tick the box. Then:
- Only you can see your feedback. Other students never see it.
- The owner sees it with a random code instead of your name or email.
- Do not write passwords, phone numbers or other personal details (email addresses and phone numbers are removed
  automatically).
- You can withdraw any entry, or all of them, from the list at the bottom of the page.

Honest answers, including "this was confusing", are the most useful.

If the page says feedback is not open, the pilot is not collecting feedback right now. You can still withdraw earlier
entries.

# Career Guide

- **Career** (`/career`): roles are matched against the skills on your profile. A role at 0% means none of your listed
  skills match it yet. Match percentages describe skill overlap, not your chance of being hired.
- **Resume** (`/resume`): a new resume is pre-filled from your profile (education, skills, projects). Edit it, then
  export it as PDF. Check every detail before you use it.
- **Jobs Discovery** (`/career/jobs`): shows real listings from live job feeds only. If the list is empty, no feed could be
  reached; jobs are never made up.
- **Internships / Applications** (`/applications`): track where you applied and the status. Private to you.
- **Mock Interviews**: practice sessions. AI feedback needs AI to be on.
- **Connected Platforms / External Profiles**: link your GitHub, LeetCode and similar profiles. "LINKED" means you saved a
  link; "CONNECTED" means it was verified.

Troubleshooting: if Career says you have no skills, add them in Student Profile > Edit > Skills.

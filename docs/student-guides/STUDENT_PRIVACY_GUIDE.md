# Privacy Guide (pilot)

What we collect: your account (name, email, password handled securely), your academic details, and what you choose to add
(skills, projects, goals). Phone, photo and CGPA are optional. Feedback and usage analytics are optional too.

Who sees what:
- Your notes, documents, code, datasets, goals, applications, resumes and feedback are **private to you**.
- Community posts show your name and the post, never your email, phone or grades.
- Profile visibility: Settings > Privacy & Data > Strictly Private or Public.

Your controls:
- Settings > Privacy & Data: visibility, usage analytics (off by default), **Download My Data**.
- Pilot Feedback: see and withdraw anything you sent.
- To leave the pilot and delete your account, tell the pilot owner. It is done within 3 working days.

Retention: pilot feedback is deleted automatically after 90 days (unless the owner set another period). Pilot accounts are
deleted 30 days after the pilot ends unless you ask to keep yours.

Concerns: menu > Profile > Pilot Feedback > **Privacy or security concern**, or contact the pilot owner directly. If you
ever see another student's private data, stop and report it at once.

Please do not upload other people's personal information, or documents you are not allowed to share.

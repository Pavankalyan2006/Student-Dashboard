# AI Tutor Guide

- Open menu > Learning > **AI Tutor** (`/ai-agent`), or the **Ask AI** button at the top.
- Ask one clear question, for example "Explain paging in operating systems with an example".
- The tutor uses your branch, semester, subjects and preferred explanation style. It does not use your contact details
  or grades.
- **Knowledge Base** (`/knowledge`): ask questions about your own uploaded documents. Answers show which document and
  page they came from. Check the citation before you rely on an answer.

Important:
- AI can be wrong. Check answers against your textbook or notes, especially for exams.
- If you see **"AI unavailable"**, AI is not switched on for this pilot server. Nothing was generated. Contact the pilot
  owner if you need it (the message mentions an "administrator"; for the pilot that is the owner).
- When AI is off, Knowledge Base questions still return the most relevant passages from your documents, labelled as
  not AI-generated.
- Do not paste passwords or other people's personal details into the chat.
